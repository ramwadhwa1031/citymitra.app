⚖️ Public & Civic Notice

Platform Disclaimer.

Transparency regarding our open civic data sources, AI processing, and independent status.

1. Non-Governmental Affiliation Notice

City Mitra (https://citymitra.app) is an independent civic tech initiative. It is not affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with the Government of India, any State Government, or any District Collectorate / Municipal Corporation.

2. Open Public Data Sourcing

Information presented across City Mitra is collected and normalized from publicly accessible government portals, official gazettes, census releases, and open government data platforms (such as data.gov.in, india.gov.in, and district websites like kurukshetra.gov.in). All administrative trademarks, emblems, and department names belong to their respective statutory owners.

3. Accuracy & Verification Disclaimer

While our data pipelines undergo continuous verification to maintain high accuracy, administrative structures, office allocations, telephone contacts, and scheme qualification criteria change periodically. Citizens are strongly encouraged to verify critical contact numbers, office hours, and scheme prerequisites directly from official government gazettes or direct departmental offices.

4. Emergency Helplines Notice

Emergency helpline numbers listed on City Mitra (e.g. Police 112, Ambulance 108, Fire 101, Women Helpline 1091) reflect national and state emergency response numbers. In life-threatening emergencies, citizens should dial national emergency hotlines directly from their telecom providers.

5. Contact for Corrections

If you notice any outdated contact numbers, officer changes, or broken links, please notify our team via corrections@citymitra.app so we can update the civic graph promptly.
