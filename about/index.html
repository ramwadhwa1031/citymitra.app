💡 Our Story & Civic Vision

Making Government
Accessible to Everyone.

City Mitra was created to solve a fundamental challenge: making public administration transparent, instant, and bilingual for every citizen in India.

👨‍💻 Founded by Ram Wadhwa 🏛️ 13 Active Districts 🇮🇳 Pan-India Civic AI
THE PROBLEM

Government information is fragmented.

In India, finding the phone number of a Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), the prerequisites for a farm subsidy, or the nearest 24×7 emergency trauma center often requires navigating dozens of outdated, unindexed websites and scanned circulars.

For citizens in Tier-2 and Tier-3 districts who speak vernacular languages, this creates an enormous barrier.

🇮🇳 ✦ 🤖

The City Mitra Solution

A centralized, bilingual AI platform that ingests, cleans, and structures district data. Citizens ask in plain Hindi, English, or Hinglish, and get deterministic, verified answers in seconds.

⚡

Instant & Deterministic

No waiting on hold or searching broken PDF links. Get direct contact numbers, office locations, and requirements.

🌐

Bilingual & Vernacular

Ask in Hindi or English, colloquial Hinglish, or voice. Technology adapted to how Indians actually communicate.

🛡️

100% Free & Privacy-First

No login required. Chats are stored locally on your device with zero server tracking or data monetization.

🗺️ OUR JOURNEY

The Evolution of City Mitra.

2024 — Inception

Born in Kurukshetra

Launched as an experimental civic chatbot for Kurukshetra district, centralizing DC office, emergency contacts, and Gita Mahotsav pilgrimage data.

2025 — Multi-State Expansion

13 Districts Across 7 States

Expanded to Fatehabad, Karnal, Ambala, Panchkula, Sirsa, Palwal, Faridabad, Dehradun, Amritsar, Varanasi, Jaipur, Lucknow, and Chandigarh.

2025 — Today

Knowledge Hub & AI Architecture

Launched the official engineering knowledge hub by Ram Wadhwa, Google Sheets civic sync, and responsive offline PWA support.

Next — The 780 Districts Vision

Pan-India Coverage

Our vision is to deploy verified district AI nodes across all 780+ districts in India.
