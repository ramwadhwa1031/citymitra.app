💡 Our Story

Making Government
Accessible to Everyone.

City Mitra was born from a simple frustration: why is finding basic government information so hard in India?

🎯 The Problem

Government info is
scattered everywhere.

Want to know your DC's phone number? Check the district website. Need emergency contacts? Try a different site. Tourist places? Yet another portal. Scheme eligibility? Good luck navigating 10 different government portals.

For 1.4 billion Indians, accessing basic civic information shouldn't require a degree in internet navigation. We built City Mitra to fix this — one AI, every answer.

✨ Our Mission

Centralize. Simplify. Empower.

Three pillars that drive everything we build.

🎯

Centralize

Bring all government data — officers, schemes, emergencies, tourism, census — into one unified, searchable platform.

🗣️

Simplify

Make it conversational. Ask in Hindi, English, or Hinglish. No forms, no navigation — just type and get answers.

🚀

Empower

Give every citizen — from farmers to students — instant access to the civic information they need, 24/7.

🛤️ Journey

Our Story So Far.

2024 — The Idea

Kurukshetra InfoBot

Started as a simple chatbot for Kurukshetra district — officers, tourist places, and emergency contacts.

2024 — Going Multi-City

City Mitra Platform

Evolved into a multi-city platform. Added Karnal, Ambala, Panchkula, Sirsa, Palwal, and Faridabad.

2025 — National Expansion

7 States, 13 Districts

Expanded beyond Haryana to Uttarakhand, Punjab, UP, Rajasthan, and Chandigarh. Added AskGita AI for Bhagavad Gita queries.

2025 — Today

AI-First Redesign

Rebuilt the entire platform with a modern AI-first interface. Added proximity alerts, voice support, and smart query routing.

Next — The Vision

Every District in India

Our goal: cover all 780+ districts across India. Make civic AI accessible to every citizen.
