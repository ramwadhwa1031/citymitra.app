❓ FAQ

Common Questions.

Is City Mitra free to use? +
Yes! City Mitra is completely free for all citizens. No sign-up, no subscription, no hidden charges. Just pick your city and start asking.
How accurate is the information? +
We source all data from official government websites, district portals, and verified records. However, as an AI system, we recommend verifying critical information like emergency contacts and timings.
Can I use City Mitra in Hindi? +
Absolutely! City Mitra supports both Hindi and English. You can even ask in Hinglish (mixed language) and the AI will understand your query perfectly.
How can I add my city? +
Click the "Request a city addition" button on any page, or submit the form above. We prioritize cities based on demand and data availability.
Is my chat data stored? +
Chat history is stored locally on your device browser (localStorage). We don't store your conversations on remote servers. You can clear your chat history anytime.
Can I install City Mitra as an app? +
Yes! City Mitra is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On mobile, tap "Add to Home Screen" in your browser menu. It works offline for cached queries.
